### Shared edit prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent PNG product artwork
for a compact phone shopping card. Input image is the edit target.

Insert the subject instruction below. Remove the entire photographic backdrop,
table surface, plinths, blocks, podiums and platform supports so only the listed
products remain. Preserve their realistic appearance, colors and materials,
and their relative proportions. Rearrange only enough to form one cohesive,
compact square-ish product cluster with clear silhouettes, tightly centered in
the canvas and a modest transparent margin. Products may naturally overlap.
Use a truly transparent alpha background everywhere around and between objects,
including visible transparency through clear glass. Very subtle natural contact
shadows only; no cast rectangular background or fog. No text, labels, logos,
frames, gradients, checkered pattern or watermark. This artwork will be placed
over colored UI backgrounds with text outside it.
